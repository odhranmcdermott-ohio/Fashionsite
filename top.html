Top Piece

Boneframe Top

The Boneframe Top is the central clothing piece in the collection. It is inspired by the structure of bones and the way a skeleton creates the hidden frame of the body.

Design Concept

This piece would use bone-like lines across the chest, shoulders, and arms to create the feeling of a visible frame.

Materials & Colors

The top would use dark fabric as the base, with ivory or bone-colored stitching, prints, or panels.
