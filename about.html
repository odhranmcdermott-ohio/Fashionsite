About the Class

The Story Behind the Collection

O’Rineveer was created as part of a fashion design class project focused on building a complete brand identity and collection. Our group wanted to create something darker and more original than regular fall fashion, so we combined autumn colors, skull imagery, skeleton designs, and hand-painted clothing.

The collection is built around the idea of fall, decay, bones, and identity. Each piece uses either skull or ribcage imagery to connect fashion with the structure underneath the body. The result is a collection that feels mysterious, rough, expressive, and connected to colder weather.

Class Project Goals

In this project, we learned how a fashion brand is built from the ground up. We had to think about the brand name, theme, product designs, visual identity, audience, website, and presentation. Instead of only making clothing, we created a full brand experience.

Our Theme

Our main theme is fall and bones. Fall connects to change, death, nature, cold weather, and decay. Bones connect to structure, identity, strength, and the hidden shape of the body. Together, these ideas create a brand that feels both natural and urban.

The Four Pieces

Black Bones Sweater

A black sweater with painted bone graphics. It creates a darker streetwear version of the skeleton theme.

White Bones Sweater

A white sweater with black skeletal details. It gives the collection a brighter, colder, ghostlike contrast.

Black Skull Balaclava

A black balaclava with skull-inspired markings. It represents mystery, protection, and identity.

White Skull Balaclava

A white balaclava with a skull-like face design. It creates a bold and eerie look for the collection.

Visual Inspiration

Our visual inspiration includes skeleton trees, skulls, ribs, bones, rough brush strokes, fall leaves, dark streets, black fabric, white fabric, burnt orange, ivory, brown, and deep red. The hand-painted style makes each piece feel personal and imperfect, which matches the brand’s mood.

What We Learned

Through this class project, we learned that fashion is not just about clothing. It is also about storytelling. A strong brand needs a clear theme, a specific mood, and a reason behind every design choice. O’Rineveer uses bones and fall imagery to turn simple clothing pieces into a full concept.
