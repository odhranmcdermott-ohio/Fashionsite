About the Class

The Story Behind the Collection

O’Rineveer was created as part of a fashion design class project focused on building a complete brand identity and collection. Our group wanted to create something that felt darker and more original than regular fall fashion, so we combined autumn colors with bone-inspired imagery, urban style, and hand-drawn visual ideas.

The brand is based on the feeling of moving through the city during fall, surrounded by cold weather, shadows, concrete, fallen leaves, and quiet streets. The clothing is meant to feel mysterious, sharp, and expressive.

Class Project Goals

In this project, we are learning how fashion brands are created from the ground up. Instead of only designing clothes, we are thinking about the full identity of a brand: the name, theme, colors, mood, target audience, product pieces, and website presentation.

Our Theme

Our main theme is fall and bones. Fall connects to change, decay, nature, and colder weather. Bones connect to structure, identity, strength, and the hidden form underneath the body.

The Four Pieces

Boneframe Top

The top piece is inspired by skeletal structure and layering.

Veil of the City

The balaclava piece adds mystery, warmth, and protection.

Relic Carrier

The bag piece brings utility into the collection.

Marrow Scarf

The scarf piece completes the fall look with warmth and texture.

Visual Inspiration

Our visual inspiration includes skeleton trees, fallen leaves, rough textures, dark streets, old walls, abstract artwork, bones, and autumn colors like burnt orange, brown, ivory, black, and deep red.

What We Learned

Through this class project, we learned that fashion is not just about making clothes. It is also about storytelling. A strong brand needs a clear theme, a specific mood, and a reason behind each design choice.
